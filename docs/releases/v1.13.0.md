# TokenFuse 1.13.0

The built-in price book holds the vendors' prices as they read on 2026-10-13, and a
model sold at two rates by prompt length is now priced by the prompt each call sent.

- Claude Haiku 5.5, the GPT-6 and GPT-5.6 families, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, DeepSeek,
  Kimi K3, GLM-5.3, GLM-5.1 and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview are priced at their vendors'
  rates. Each used to settle at the 15 / 75 fallback.
- A call whose prompt is above a model's threshold (Claude Haiku 5.5: 100,000 tokens;
  OpenAI's tiered models: 272,000; Gemini 2.5 Pro and 3.1 Pro Preview: 200,000) is
  priced whole at the long rate, and every shorter call at the base rate.
- A GPT-5.6 or later cache write is priced as a cache write, not as plain input.

Nothing is renamed or removed and no environment name is added. The price book's new
`long_context` list in `contracts/tokenfuse-constants.json` and the price-book route's
`long_context` fields are additive, as `compat/1.0.json` records. The umbrella crate and
both client SDKs move to 1.13.0 with no client behavior change.

## Added

- **Prompt-length tiers (#416, invariant 99).** A model may carry one long-prompt
  rate. When a call's prompt (input, cache reads and cache writes together, the way the
  vendors count it) is above the model's threshold, every part of the call, output
  included, is priced at the long rate; at or below it, at the base rate. The estimate
  that reserves a call's budget and the settlement both use the same rule. Fourteen ids
  carry a tier: Claude Haiku 5.5 on the Claude API, Bedrock and OpenRouter; gpt-6-astra,
  gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-sol, gpt-6-luna, gpt-5.6-sol, gpt-5.6-terra, gpt-5.6-luna, gpt-5.5
  and gpt-5.4; Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview on Vertex AI.
- **Rows for the current models.** Claude Haiku 5.5 (`claude-haiku-5-5`,
  `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`, and `anthropic.claude-haiku-5-5` at the regional rate);
  thirteen OpenAI ids at the Standard tier; `deepseek-flash` and its two legacy names
  and `deepseek-v4-pro`; `kimi-k3`; `glm-5.3` and `glm-5.1`;
  `google/gemini-3.1-pro-preview`. Each group names the page and the date its rates
  were read.
- **The tiers are published and reported.** `price_book.long_context` lists each tier
  with its threshold and five rate columns; `price_book.models` keeps every id's base
  rate in its existing shape. `GET /v1/price-book` adds `long_context` (the tiers in
  effect) and `long_context_dropped_models`.

## Fixed

- **Claude Sonnet 5.5's cache hits** are priced at 0.10 per million tokens, Anthropic's
  0.05x of input. The row shared Sonnet 5's 0.20.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5** had no row and settled at the 15 / 75 fallback, about 150 times
  its price.
- **An OpenAI cache write** reported as `prompt_tokens_details.cache_write_tokens` is
  read as a cache write and taken out of the input count, where it was priced as plain
  input, 20 percent under what OpenAI bills for GPT-5.6 and later. A usage that reports
  no cache writes is read exactly as before.

## Changed

- **Gemini 2.5 Pro** is priced 1.25 / 10.00 (cached 0.125) up to 200,000 prompt tokens
  and 2.50 / 15.00 (cached 0.25) above, where every call used to pay the higher rate.

## How the book chooses where a vendor's page leaves a choice

- **Endpoint.** An id that names its endpoint is priced for it. Where the caller must
  choose the endpoint and the id does not say (a bare Bedrock id, a Google Cloud dated
  id, every Gemini id on Vertex AI), the row is the dearer endpoint. A first-party API
  whose default is global (Claude API, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Z.ai) is priced at
  that default; an opt-in regional uplift is the operator file's to set.
- **Dated prices.** The book has no effective date. Where a vendor lists two dated
  rates for one id, the row is the dearer, so passing the date never leaves it
  under-charging: Gemini 3.6, 3.7 and 3.8 Flash at the standard rate (the introductory
  rate is paid as a credit back), gpt-5.6-sol at the table's 4 / 20.
- **Time of day.** DeepSeek bills half at off-peak hours; the row is the peak rate.
- **OpenRouter.** Rows are added for Anthropic models only. Kimi, GLM and OpenAI models
  on OpenRouter are served by many providers at rates far apart, so those ids keep the
  fallback.
- **The fallback** stays 15 / 75. gpt-6-astra's long rate is above it in input, cache
  read and cache write, and is named as an exception in the test that holds the
  fallback above every row, as o1's cache read already was.

## Upgrade

- **Calls on the newly listed ids are charged at the vendor's rate.** Their answers
  carry `x-fuse-price: known` where they carried `fallback`, and they cost a fraction
  of what they did, so a budget sized against fallback-priced calls now admits more of
  them.
- **An operator row for a tiered id drops that id's tier.** A `TOKENFUSE_PRICE_BOOK`
  row is the whole price for its id at every prompt length. The startup line counts
  the dropped tiers (`long_context_dropped=`) and `GET /v1/price-book` names them.
  The file's shape is unchanged.
- **The launchers.** stack-single and stack-k8s pin TokenFuse 1.11.0 and are unaffected
  until their pin moves; stack-up builds TokenFuse from source and takes this release
  with its next build.

## Not proven

- No live provider call was made at the new rates. The rates were read from the
  vendors' pages on 2026-10-13 and are true on that date and no other; the behavior
  was measured by tests in CI, including one through the gateway with a real HTTP
  provider against a local upstream.
- A Responses API usage object (`input_tokens_details`) is not parsed for cache reads
  or writes; the gateway serves the Messages and Chat Completions shapes.
- The pre-flight estimate picks a tier from the estimated prompt; a prompt estimated
  under a threshold that settles above it is reserved at the base rate and settled at
  the long one.
- verdryx's mirror of the book and estate-gates C3 compare the base rows; nothing
  outside this repository checks the tiers.
